Sky Jellyfish: a colossal bioluminescent jellyfish, as tall as a skyscraper, drifts low over a night highway, its glowing cyan-and-magenta tentacles trailing light above tiny cars with streaking headlights. A creature of the deep sea in the wrong place entirely, at the wrong scale, caught mid-drift — the jolt of seeing something impossible hover over something utterly ordinary.

## Why this image

The idea is a wrong-place, wrong-scale collision: a deep-sea animal the size of a tower floating over a highway at night, so the excitement comes from the scene itself, not from a caption. The surprise is the first read, the jellyfish is enormous and luminous against a dark road full of small ordinary cars, and the momentum is in the light trails and the cars rushing underneath, which keep the eye moving from the glow down to the street. The craft is in keeping that contrast clean: one creature, one road, two glowing colours, no clutter, so the whole composition still reads at 300px, where the silhouette and the glow do all the work.
